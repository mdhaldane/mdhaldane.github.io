![Me](/assets/images/mdh_ai.jpg){: width="400" .align-left} I am an editor with [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AXyLY3I-ufo/matt-haldane)'s crypto and digital payments team. My work sits at the intersection of emerging technology and its impact on global society — from the complexities of blockchain and AI to the geopolitical tensions of the semiconductor industry.

### From Systems to Stories

I spent four years as an IT specialist in the US Navy, based in Asia-Pacific. I had a ground-level view of how technology functions in the push-and-pull between the US and China in the Pacific theater. Fascinated by the rapid economic and digital transformation of the region, I would eventually move to Beijing to pursue a master's degree.

My time in China brought me to Shenzhen, then to Hong Kong, before I left the country and settled in Singapore.

---

### What I'm Following

My daily work is focused on digital finance, but I also have a deep interest in:
- **Digital sovereignty:** How countries and individuals navigate the "splinternet."
- **Emerging hardware:** The evolution of gadgets and the silicon that powers them.
- **Visual storytelling:** Incorporating data into digital news.
